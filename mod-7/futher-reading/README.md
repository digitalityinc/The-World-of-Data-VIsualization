# 📚 Further Reading (Module 7)

This list is **optional** — it’s here if you want to explore how data visualization is used in real healthcare settings.
You don’t need to read everything. If you only pick one, start with the **survey paper**.

---

## 🧾 Research Survey (Recommended)
A deeper, research-focused look at the field of healthcare data visualization.

- [Data visualization in healthcare and medicine: a survey (The Visual Computer)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00371-024-03586-x)  
  A comprehensive review summarizing 76 articles on visualization techniques, algorithms, and future research directions in healthcare data visualization.

---

## 🏥 Industry Examples (Optional)
A practical overview of how visualization supports real workflows like dashboards and decision-making.

- [Healthcare Data Visualization: Examples & Key Benefits (Demigos)](https://demigos.com/blog-post/healthcare-data-visualization/)  
  An industry-focused overview of how visualized healthcare data—such as dashboards, infographics, and interactive tools—improves patient care, operational efficiency, and decision-making.
