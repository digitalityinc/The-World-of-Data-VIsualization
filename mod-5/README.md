## 🎯 Learning Objectives
By the end of this week, students should be able to:
- Develop a deeper understanding of **Pandas DataFrame**.  
- Explore and visualize data in Pandas to answer key questions:  
  - What are the **major properties/features** of this dataset?  
  - What are the **expected types** (int, float, string, boolean, etc.)?  
  - Is there obvious **missing data**, and can Pandas detect them?  
  - Are there other types of missing data **not immediately visible** (undetectable by Pandas)?
